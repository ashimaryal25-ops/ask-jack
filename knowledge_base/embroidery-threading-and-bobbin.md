# Janome MC550E — Threading & Bobbin Guide
machine: Janome MC550E
category: embroidery — threading

## Overview
Proper upper threading and correct bobbin insertion are essential for smooth embroidery. If the upper thread is not seated properly in the tension discs or take-up lever, the thread will snap or form a large knot (birdnest) under your fabric.

---

## 1. High-Tension Bobbin Holder for Embroidery (Yellow Dot)

The Janome MC550E comes with a specialized **high-tension bobbin holder marked with a yellow dot (①)** specifically designed for embroidery bobbin thread.

[IMAGE: https://xyaewwhcelutcoosbawk.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/images/embroidery/chunk-high-tension-bobbin-holder.png | High Tension Bobbin Holder with Yellow Dot for Embroidery]

### How to verify or swap the bobbin holder:
1. Turn off the machine power switch or press the lockout key.
2. Remove the needle, embroidery foot, and needle plate (using the screwdriver key).
3. Take out the standard bobbin holder (②).
4. Insert the special embroidery bobbin holder with the **yellow dot** (⑤) into the hook race.
5. Align the knob of the bobbin holder (③) with the stopper on the machine (④).
6. Replace the needle plate, presser foot, and needle.

---

## 2. Winding the Bobbin

1. Place the spool of bobbin thread on the extra spool pin.
2. Draw the thread around the bobbin winder thread guide.
3. Pass the thread through the hole in the bobbin from the inside to the outside.
4. Put the bobbin onto the bobbin winder shaft.
5. Push the bobbin winder shaft to the right against the bobbin stopper.
6. Hold the free end of the thread and press the **Start/Stop button**.
7. Stop the machine after the bobbin has wound a few layers, trim the thread tail close to the hole, and press Start/Stop again.
8. When the bobbin is fully wound, it stops automatically. Push the shaft back to the left, remove the bobbin, and snip the thread.

[IMAGE: https://xyaewwhcelutcoosbawk.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/images/embroidery/diagram-winding-bobbin.png | Winding the Bobbin Diagram]

## 3. Inserting the Bobbin into the Hook Race

1. Slide the hook cover release button to the right and lift off the transparent plastic hook cover plate.
2. Place the wound bobbin in the bobbin holder with the thread running **counterclockwise** (forming a letter "P" shape).
3. Hold the bobbin gently with your fingertip so it doesn't spin.
4. Guide the thread into the front slit (1) on the front-left edge of the bobbin holder.
5. Pull the thread to the left and follow the curved guide channel (2).
6. Pull the thread toward you along the cutting blade guide (3) and pull down to cut the excess thread cleanly.
7. Reattach the hook cover plate. (You do NOT need to draw up the bobbin thread manually — the machine will pick it up automatically).

[IMAGE: https://xyaewwhcelutcoosbawk.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/images/embroidery/diagram-inserting-bobbin.png | Inserting the Bobbin into Hook Race]

## 4. Setting the Spool of Embroidery Thread

1. Raise the spool pin. Place the spool of embroidery thread on the spool pin.
2. Choose the correct spool holder cap based on the size of the spool:
   - **Large/Medium holder**: For standard embroidery thread cones and spools.
   - **Small holder**: For narrow spools.
   - **Special holder**: For small spools with a narrow bore.
3. Press the spool holder firmly against the thread spool so thread unspools smoothly without wobbling.

[IMAGE: https://xyaewwhcelutcoosbawk.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/images/embroidery/diagram-spool-setup.png | Setting the Spool of Thread and Spool Holders]

## 5. Upper Threading (Steps 1 to 7)

**CRITICAL RULE**: Always make sure the **presser foot is in the UP position** while threading. If the presser foot is down, the internal tension discs remain closed, the thread will not seat inside them, and severe bunching will occur under the fabric.

1. **Step 1**: Draw the thread from the spool through the upper thread guide (1).
2. **Step 2**: Pass the thread under the pre-tension thread guide (2).
3. **Step 3**: Draw the thread down along the right-hand channel (3).
4. **Step 4**: Draw the thread around the bottom turn and up along the left-hand channel (4).
5. **Step 5**: Pass the thread through the eyelet of the thread take-up lever from right to left (5). Ensure the thread is firmly seated inside the take-up lever hole!
6. **Step 6**: Draw the thread down through the left channel and slip it behind the needle bar thread guide from the right (6).
7. **Step 7**: Thread the needle using the built-in needle threader or manually from front to back.

[IMAGE: https://xyaewwhcelutcoosbawk.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/images/embroidery/diagram-upper-threading.png | Janome MC550E Upper Threading Steps 1 to 7]

## 6. Using the Built-in Needle Threader

1. Turn the handwheel toward you until the needle is at its highest point, or press the needle up/down key.
2. Turn off the power switch or press the lockout key for safety.
3. Pull the needle threader knob down as far as it will go. The small threader hook will pass through the eye of the needle from back to front.
4. Draw the thread around the guide and under the hook from left to right.
5. Gently release the knob. The hook will pull a loop of thread through the needle eye to the back.
6. Pull the loop out through the rear of the needle to complete threading.
