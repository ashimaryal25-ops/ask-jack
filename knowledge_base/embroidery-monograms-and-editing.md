# Janome MC550E — Monograms, Lettering & On-Screen Editing
machine: Janome MC550E
category: embroidery — editing

## Overview
The Janome MC550E allows you to create custom lettering, monograms, and names directly on the machine without using external software. You can also edit, rotate, resize, and position any imported `.JEF` design directly on the visual touch screen.

---

## 1. Creating Monograms & Built-in Lettering

### Available Built-in Fonts:
- **Gothic Font**: Clean, modern sans-serif lettering (great for names, labels, and sportswear).
- **Script Font**: Cursive elegant typography (ideal for gifts, initials, and formal apparel).
- **Cheltenham Font**: Classic serif typography.
- **2-Letter / 3-Letter Monograms**: Traditional monogram layouts with decorative border frames.

### How to Program a Name or Monogram on Screen:
1. Turn on the machine and tap the **Monogram icon (A/B)** on the opening screen.
2. Select your desired font style and letter size (Small, Medium, or Large).
3. Type your text using the on-screen keyboard.
4. To create a monogram with border frames, select the 2-Letter or 3-Letter monogram template.
5. Tap **OK** to load your text into the Ready to Sew window.

---

## 2. Monogramming in an Arc (Curved Text)

You can curve your text in an upward or downward arc (for example, lettering arched across a jacket back or cap):

1. In the editing window, select your monogram text.
2. Tap the **Arc Key** (the curved text icon).
3. Tap the **Upper Arc** or **Lower Arc** button to set the curve direction.
4. Tap the **Arc Angle adjustment buttons** to widen or tighten the curvature.
5. Tap **OK** to apply the curve.

---

## 3. On-Screen Design Editing & Layout Tools

Before pressing Start, you can adjust your design directly in the machine's Editing Mode:

- **Resizing**: Tap the **Resize Key** to scale the design between 80% and 120% of its original size while preserving stitch density.
- **Rotating**: Tap the **Rotate Key** to turn the design in 1-degree or 45-degree increments.
- **Flipping / Mirroring**: Tap the **Horizontal Flip** or **Vertical Flip** key to mirror your design.
- **Centering**: Tap the **Center Position Key** to automatically center the design within the active hoop.
- **Grouping Patterns**: Combine multiple separate designs or text blocks onto a single hoop so they sew sequentially.
- **Single Color Sewing**: If you want a multi-color design to sew completely in a single thread color without pausing between color stops, tap the **Single Color Key**.
