# Embroidery Troubleshooting & Error Diagnosis
machine: Janome MC550E / MC9850
category: embroidery — troubleshooting

## Quick Diagnosis Matrix

Find your symptom below for the immediate root cause and verified ICL fix:

---

## 1. "Birdnesting" (Massive Tangle / Knot of Thread Under the Fabric)
- **Symptom**: The machine sews a few stitches, then jams violently with a huge clump of thread underneath the hoop on the bobbin side.
- **Cause**: The **upper thread has zero tension**. This happens 99% of the time because the machine was threaded with the **presser foot down**, meaning the internal tension discs never opened to grip the thread. It can also happen if the thread slipped out of the take-up lever (step 5).
- **Fix**:
  1. Stop machine immediately and cut the fabric free with snips.
  2. Clear all tangled threads from the hook area.
  3. **Raise the presser foot**.
  4. Rethread the entire upper thread path from step 1 through 7, making sure the thread is firmly seated inside the take-up lever eyelet.

## 2. Upper Thread Snapping / Fraying
- **Symptom**: The top thread repeatedly snaps or shreds during stitching.
- **Checklist & Fixes**:
  1. **Spool notch**: Check if thread is catching on the slit/notch of the plastic spool. Use a spool holder cap larger than the spool or flip the spool.
  2. **Burred/Dull needle**: Replace the needle with a new **Blue Tip (#11)** or **Purple Tip (#14)** needle.
  3. **Incorrect needle orientation**: Ensure the flat side of the needle faces the REAR (away from you).
  4. **Thread path**: Verify thread passes through all guides 1 to 7 without looping around the spool pin.

[IMAGE: https://xyaewwhcelutcoosbawk.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/images/embroidery/diagram-replacing-needle.png | Replacing Needles and Needle Orientation]

## 3. Fabric Puckering or Outlines Misaligned
- **Symptom**: The fabric wrinkles and bunches around the stitches, or the satin border does not line up with the color fill.
- **Cause**: Inadequate stabilizer or improper hooping tension.
- **Fixes**:
  1. **Fabric in hoop was too loose**: Hoop the fabric taut like a drum skin.
  2. **Wrong stabilizer**: Stretchy knits (t-shirts, hoodies) MUST use **Cut-Away** stabilizer. Tear-away will stretch and cause gaps.
  3. **Stitch density too high**: In Artistic Digitizer, reduce stitch density or enable automatic underlay stitching to anchor the fabric.

---

## 4. Skipped Stitches
- **Symptom**: Gaps or missing stitches appear in the design.
- **Fix**:
  1. Needle is bent, dull, or wrong type.
  2. For knits/synthetics, install a **Purple Tip needle** (ballpoint cobra head prevents skipped stitches).
  3. For woven fabrics, install a **Blue Tip needle**.

---

## 5. Screen Warning: "Lower the Presser Foot"
- **Symptom**: Machine beeps and refuses to start when you press the Start/Stop button.
- **Fix**: Lower the black presser foot lever located behind the needle bar before pressing Start.

[IMAGE: https://xyaewwhcelutcoosbawk.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/images/embroidery/diagram-machine-operating-buttons.png | Machine Operating Buttons and Presser Foot Warning]

---

## 6. Needle Striking the Hoop / Carriage Obstruction
- **Symptom**: Grinding sound or error message about carriage movement.
- **Fix**:
  1. Immediately turn off the machine.
  2. Always use the **Trace function** on screen before starting any job to verify design boundaries.
  3. Ensure no objects (scissors, garments, cords) are blocking the movement of the embroidery carriage arm.
  4. If the carriage is stuck, speak with ICL staff (Josh or Eric).
