# Janome MC550E & MC9850 — Overview
machine: Janome MC550E / MC9850
category: embroidery — overview
location: Design Studio, Plank 117, Gettysburg College ICL

## What are these machines?
The Janome Memory Craft 550E (MC550E) and Janome MC9850 are computer-controlled automatic embroidery machines available to all students at the ICL. They allow you to turn vector graphics, illustrations, and logos into precision stitched designs on fabric, garments, hats, patches, and accessories.

[IMAGE: https://xyaewwhcelutcoosbawk.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/images/embroidery/diagram-machine-parts.png | Janome MC550E Parts Overview and Visual Touch Screen]

## What can you make?
- Custom apparel: embroidered hoodies, sweatshirts, t-shirts, polo shirts, jackets
- Accessories: tote bags, beanies, caps, backpacks, blankets, towels
- Patches: custom embroidered iron-on or sew-on patches
- Monograms & typography: names, initials, club logos, event branding

## Machine Specifications & Hoop Sizes

### Janome MC550E (Dedicated Large-Format Embroidery Machine)
- Max embroidery area: 200mm × 360mm (7.9" × 14.2")
- Available hoops:
  - **SQ14b**: 140mm × 140mm (5.5" × 5.5") — standard chest logos, patches
  - **RE20b**: 200mm × 140mm (7.9" × 5.5") — rectangular designs
  - **SQ20b**: 200mm × 200mm (7.9" × 7.9") — large back designs, tote bags
  - **RE36b**: 200mm × 360mm (7.9" × 14.2") — extra-large banners, jacket backs
- Sewing speed: 400–860 stitches per minute
- Display: 5-inch full-color visual touch screen

### Janome MC9850 (Compact Embroidery Machine)
- Available hoops:
  - **FA10a**: 40mm × 100mm (1.6" × 3.9") — small sleeve logos, pockets, cuffs
  - **SQ14a**: 5.5" × 5.5" (140mm × 140mm) — chest logos, small graphics
  - **RE20a**: 170mm × 200mm (6.7" × 7.9") — medium graphics

## File Format & USB Requirements
- File format: **.JEF** (Janome Embroidery Format).
- USB folder structure: Files MUST be saved on a USB drive inside a top-level folder named `EMB`, and within that a folder named `EMBF` (e.g. `USB:/EMB/EMBF/mydesign.JEF`). The machine will not detect files saved in the root folder.

## Software Used in the Lab
1. **VinylMasterCut**: For vectorizing bitmap images (PNG/JPG) into clean vector lines (`.SVG`).
2. **Artistic Digitizer**: For converting vector artwork into embroidery stitch patterns, setting density, and exporting to `.JEF`.
