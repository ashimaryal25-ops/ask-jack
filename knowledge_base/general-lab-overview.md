## What is the ICL?
The Innovation & Creativity Lab (ICL) is Gettysburg College's dedicated makerspace and digital fabrication studio, located in **Plank Hall, Room 117**. The lab provides tools, machines, materials, and guidance to turn ideas into physical prototypes, art, functional parts, and personalized products.

## Who is Jack?
Jack is the AI assistant built specifically for the ICL to help students, faculty, and staff use equipment safely and step-by-step, even when no instructor is around.
Jack is named after **Clarence B. "Jack" Rogers Jr. (Class of 1951)**, the Gettysburg College alumnus whose philanthropy and vision for technology education made the lab possible.

## Equipment Available in the Lab

### 1. 3D Printers
- **Creality Ender 3 V3 KE**: High-speed desktop FDM printers (220 × 220 × 240mm build volume) for standard plastic parts, prototypes, phone stands, and figurines using PLA/PETG/TPU.
- **Creality CR-M4**: Large-format 3D printer (450 × 450 × 470mm build volume) for oversized projects.

### 2. Automatic Embroidery Machines
- **Janome Memory Craft 550E**: Large-format digital embroidery machine (up to 7.9" × 14.2" / 200 × 360mm area) for jackets, hoodies, hats, tote bags, and custom patches.
- **Janome MC9850**: Compact digital embroidery machine with hoop sizes FA10a (1.6"×3.9"), SQ14a (5.5"×5.5"), and RE20a (6.7"×7.9").
- **Software**: VinylMasterCut (image vectorization to SVG) and Artistic Digitizer (converting SVG to .JEF stitch files).

### 3. Laser Cutters & Vinyl Cutters
- Precision CO2 laser cutting and engraving for acrylic, wood, leather, and cardboard.
- Vinyl plotters for custom stickers, heat-transfer vinyl (HTV) for apparel, and decal graphics.

### 4. Electronics, Soldering & Hand Tools
- Soldering irons, multimeters, wire strippers, heat guns, hand tools, fasteners, and assembly benches.
