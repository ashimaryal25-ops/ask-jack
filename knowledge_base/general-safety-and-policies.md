## General Safety Rules

1. **Eye Protection**: Always wear ANSI-approved safety glasses when operating 3D printers, rotary tools, soldering irons, or when observing high-speed machinery.
2. **Clothing & Hair**:
   - Tie back long hair and tuck in loose jewelry or lanyards before operating rotating equipment.
   - Closed-toe shoes are required at all times in Plank 117.
3. **Never Leave Critical Processes Unattended**:
   - For 3D printing: You must stay and observe the first 2–3 layers (first 5 minutes) to ensure proper bed adhesion before stepping away. Always write your name and estimated finish time on the dry-erase board.
   - For embroidery: Remain within earshot of the machine during stitching. If thread breaks or hoop collides, press Start/Stop immediately.
4. **Hot Surfaces & Mechanical Hazards**:
   - 3D printer nozzles reach 200°C–260°C and heated beds reach 60°C–100°C. Never touch active hotends.
   - Keep hands and foreign objects away from moving embroidery carriages, lead screws, and belts.
5. **Medical Emergencies**:
   - If an injury occurs (burn, puncture, severe cut), immediately inform ICL staff or call Campus Safety (x6911) / Health Services. Jack cannot provide medical advice.

## Material Policies
- Only use lab-approved materials: PLA, PETG, and TPU for 3D printers; standard polyester/rayon embroidery thread with appropriate stabilizers for embroidery.
- Do NOT bring unapproved plastics (such as PVC, ABS with halogenated additives, or unknown resins) into laser cutters or 3D printers.

## Cleanliness & Community Guidelines
- Clean up your workspace after every job: scrap filament, trimmed thread tails, tear-away backing, and support material must be placed in the designated trash or recycling bins.
- Return shared hand tools, snips, screwdriver keys, and USB drives to their designated holders.
- Erase your name from the job board when your print is collected.
