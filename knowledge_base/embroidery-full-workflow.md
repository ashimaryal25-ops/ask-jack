# Janome MC550E & MC9850 — Complete Beginner Workflow
machine: Janome MC550E / MC9850
category: embroidery — full workflow

## "I want to embroider something. Where do I start?"

Here is the complete step-by-step process from digital design to finished embroidered fabric at the ICL.

---

## Step 1 — Vectorize your image (if you have a bitmap PNG/JPG)

If you already have a clean `.SVG` vector file, skip to Step 2.

1. Open **VinylMasterCut** on the lab computer.
2. Import your image (`.png` or `.jpg`).
3. Click the **Vectorize** button.
4. Choose the number of colors you want in the trace.
5. Click **Trace**, then click **Accept**.
6. Go to **File > Export** and save your image as an **.SVG** file.

[IMAGE: https://xyaewwhcelutcoosbawk.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/images/embroidery/icl-vectorize-vinylmaster.png | Vectorizing an Image in VinylMasterCut]

---

## Step 2 — Create the design in Artistic Digitizer

1. Open **Artistic Digitizer** on the desktop and select **Create New**.
2. Select your machine (**Janome MC550E** or **MC9850**) and choose your hoop size.
3. Go to **File > Import** and select your `.SVG` file.
4. Resize and position your design within the hoop boundary lines.
5. Go to **File > Save As**.
6. Select **.JEF** as the file type.
7. Save the `.JEF` file to your USB drive inside `EMB/EMBF` (e.g. `USB:/EMB/EMBF/my_design.JEF`).
8. Safely eject the USB drive.

[IMAGE: https://xyaewwhcelutcoosbawk.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/images/embroidery/icl-artistic-digitizer-save.png | Setting Up Artistic Digitizer and Saving to USB EMB/EMBF]

---

## Step 3 — Choose stabilizer and hoop your fabric

1. Select the correct stabilizer for your fabric:
   - **Cut-Away**: For stretchy or knit fabrics (hoodies, t-shirts, sweatshirts).
   - **Tear-Away**: For stable, non-stretchy wovens (canvas, denim, leather, towels).
   - **Water-Soluble Sticky Back**: For collars, cuffs, or hoopless sticky mounting.
   - **Water-Soluble Topping**: Place on top of fuzzy/textured fabrics (fleece, corduroy, terry towels).
2. Separate the inner hoop from the outer hoop by loosening the screw.
3. Place the stabilizer underneath the fabric and sandwich both between the inner and outer hoops.
4. Push the inner hoop down into the outer hoop until flush, and tighten the screw so the fabric is taut like a drum skin without stretching the grain.

[IMAGE: https://xyaewwhcelutcoosbawk.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/images/embroidery/diagram-hooping-fabric.png | Setting Fabric in an Embroidery Hoop with Stabilizer]

---

## Step 4 — Thread the machine and insert the bobbin

1. Ensure the high-tension embroidery bobbin holder (marked with a **yellow dot**) is installed in the hook race.
2. Drop the wound bobbin into the hook race so thread unwinds counterclockwise (like a 'P'). Guide thread into the front slit, pull around the tension guide, and snip on the blade.
3. Turn on the machine and raise the presser foot.
4. Place your embroidery thread spool on the spool pin with a spool holder cap.
5. Follow the numbered thread guides 1 through 7:
   - Guide 1 (upper guide) → Guide 2 (pre-tension) → Channel 3 down → Turn 4 around → Take-up lever 5 (right to left) → Needle bar guide 6.
6. Lower the built-in needle threader lever to pull the thread through the needle eye (step 7).

[IMAGE: https://xyaewwhcelutcoosbawk.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/images/embroidery/diagram-upper-threading.png | Janome MC550E Upper Threading 1-7]
[IMAGE: https://xyaewwhcelutcoosbawk.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/images/embroidery/diagram-inserting-bobbin.png | Inserting the Bobbin into Hook Race]

## Step 5 — Attach the hoop to the embroidery arm

1. Raise the presser foot lifter.
2. Slide the hooped fabric under the needle and presser foot.
3. Align the mounting pins on the hoop bracket with the carriage arm.
4. Push the clamp lever down to lock the hoop securely into the machine carriage.

[IMAGE: https://xyaewwhcelutcoosbawk.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/images/embroidery/diagram-attaching-hoop.png | Attaching the Embroidery Hoop to the Machine Carriage Arm]

---

## Step 6 — Select design, trace boundary, and sew

1. Insert your USB drive into the USB port on the right side of the machine.
2. On the touch screen, tap the **Folder icon** → tap the **USB tab** → select your `.JEF` file.
3. Tap the **Trace Key** on screen. The hoop will move along the perimeter of the design to confirm the needle will not hit the hoop frame.
4. Lower the presser foot (black lever on the right/back).
5. Hold the thread tail lightly with your fingers and press the **Start/Stop button** (button turns green to start, red while sewing).
6. After 5–6 stitches, press Start/Stop to pause. Snip the starting thread tail close to the fabric, then press Start/Stop to resume.
7. The machine will stitch automatically and pause/beep when it is time to change colors.

## Step 7 — Remove hoop and finish your embroidery
1. When stitching is complete, press the physical **Thread Cutter button** (marked with a scissor icon ② directly above the needle on the machine head) to automatically trim both the upper and bobbin threads.
2. Raise the presser foot.
3. Lift the hoop clamp lever and slide the hoop off the machine carriage.
4. Loosen the hoop screw and remove your fabric.
5. Snip any remaining jump threads on the front with embroidery scissors.
6. Gently tear or trim away excess stabilizer from the back (leaving about 1/4" margin if using cut-away stabilizer).
7. If using water-soluble stabilizer/topping, rinse with warm water or dab with a damp sponge.

[IMAGE: https://xyaewwhcelutcoosbawk.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/images/embroidery/diagram-machine-operating-buttons.png | Janome MC550E Operating Buttons — Thread Cutter Button Location]

---

## That's it. You embroidered your project!

**Something went wrong?**
- Thread snapped → re-check upper thread path and take-up lever.
- Thread bunching underneath (birdnest) → rethread upper thread with presser foot UP.
- Machine beeps "Lower Presser Foot" → lower the presser foot lever before pressing Start.
- Needle breaks or jams → turn off power, replace needle, and speak with ICL staff.
